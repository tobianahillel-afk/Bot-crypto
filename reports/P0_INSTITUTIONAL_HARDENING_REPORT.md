## Mathematical hardening

The following controls are now executable rather than merely documented:

1. finite numerical inputs are mandatory;
2. booleans are rejected as market numbers;
3. NaN and positive/negative infinity are rejected;
4. failures include a stable data-quality error code, field name and reason;
5. mathematical thresholds are stored in a versioned manifest;
6. the manifest is checksummed and validated before use;
7. RSI, Bollinger bands, rate of change and clamping are tested against exact analytical oracles;
8. property-based tests verify bounds, signs, finiteness, recent-window behavior and saturation;
9. critical numerical functions are mutation-tested with a blocking score threshold.
